2. Banana baaki Still to build

  1. Stage 5

    Kaam ek aadmi ya poori team ko, hours ke saath

    • Production step ko team ke ek aadmi ya poori team ko deta hai, aur planned hours likhta hai.
    • Step khatam hone pe system asli hours record karta hai, sirf utna time jitna har aadmi device ke hisaab se present tha.
    • Assign karte waqt dikhega aaj kaun present hai.
    • Team efficiency planned aur asli hours se nikalegi, type nahi hogi.

    Iske liye section 3 me "Hours aur cost" ke jawab chahiye.

  2. Stage 6

    Labour cost part aur machine ki price me

    • Asli hours × har aadmi ka rate (salary se) = kaam ki cost.
    • Ye cost part ki cost me, phir BOM me, phir machine ki MRP tak jaati hai, usi automatic price update se jo pehle se bana hai.
    • Aaj haath se type hone wali production cost ki jagah lega.

    Iske liye section 3 me "Hours aur cost" ke jawab chahiye.

  3. Device

    Live attendance device ka link chalu karna

    • Aapke e-timeoffice account ka link ban gaya aur test ho gaya. Aapke chune din pe saath me chalu karenge, phir har device code ko uske aadmi se match karenge.

3. Aapse sawal Questions for you

Jo bina in jawabon ke ban sakta tha, wo bana diya. Ye jawab tay karenge ki stage 5 aur 6 kaise kaam karein, aur attendance aur pay aur sahi ho.

Attendance aur pay stage 2 aur 3 ko fine-tune karne ke liye

  1. Device ke saare day status ki list bhej sakte hain? Half day, holiday, leave, aur "---CF" ka matlab kya hai. Abhi tak sirf P, A aur WO dikhe hain.
  2. Agar punch bhool gaye, sirf in ya sirf out record hua, to kya hona chahiye?
  3. Device ka overtime pay hota hai? Hota hai to kis rate pe?
  4. Sunday ya holiday pe kaam karne ki extra pay milti hai? Abhi system wo din gin ke dikhata hai, par kuch add nahi karta.
  5. Late aana half day me badalna chahiye? Jaise 3 late = aadha din.
  6. Saturday se Monday ki leave me Sunday leave balance se katna chahiye? Abhi katta hai. Pay dono tarah same hai.
  7. Koi labour daily pay pe hai, monthly ki jagah?
  8. Kya sab device pe punch karte hain, office staff aur Heads bhi? Koi field staff hai jo device tak nahi pahunchta, jaise service technician?
  9. Device me 13 log October ke end se punch nahi kar rahe. Kya wo chhod chuke hain?
  10. Factory se bahar kaam karne walon ke liye "Mark Attendance" page rehna chahiye?

Hours aur cost stage 5 aur 6 se pehle chahiye

  1. Lunch kitne baje hota hai, aur kya shift ke hisaab se alag hai? Workers lunch pe punch nahi karte, isliye pata hona chahiye ki step ke hours se kab ka time nikalna hai.
  2. Kaun kaun si shifts hain aur kaun kis shift me hai? Koi 9 ghante ki duty pe hai? Device ka data dikhata hai ki kuch log hain.
  3. Hourly rate hamesha 8 ghante pe nikale, ya har aadmi ki apni shift ke hisaab se?
  4. Ek step team ke do logon ko de sakte hain, ya sirf ek aadmi ya poori team?
  5. Poori team ki cost me supervisor bhi gina jaaye?
  6. Ek aadmi ek saath do steps pe ho to doosra rokna hai, ya uska time dono me baantna hai?
  7. Step ko pause kar sakte hain, jaise material ka wait?
  8. Absent aadmi ko assign kar sakte hain, ya sirf warning ke saath? Koi step ke beech me chhod de to?
  9. QC rejection ke baad rework ke hours price me gine jaayein?
  10. Abhi aakhri build price set karti hai. Wahi rakhein, ya pichhle kuch builds ka average?
  11. Labour cost nikalne ke baad haath se type hone wali production cost hata dein? Typed expense rahega?
  12. Labour cost kaun dekh sakta hai? Hours aur cost se kisi ki salary nikali ja sakti hai.
  13. Jis worker ki salary nahi daali, uski cost ₹0 ginegi. Price pe warning dikhaye, "Price provisional" jaisi?

Pichhle kaam ke khule sawal

  1. Accounts → Sales Invoice → "New Invoice" se koi seedha bill banata hai? Ye bina QC, dispatch ya serial number ke stock kam karta hai. Use nahi hota to chhupa denge.
  2. Material QC pass hone ke baad kharab nikle, to aaj vendor ko wapas kaise jaata hai?
  3. Outsource hua Child Part ya Machine unit QC me reject ho, to rework ke liye wapas kaise jaaye? Sub Child Part batch ka rework rasta hai, single unit ka abhi nahi.
