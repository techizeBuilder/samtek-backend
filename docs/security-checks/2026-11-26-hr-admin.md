## Findings (confirmed live by `sec-hr-probe` on made-up companies; **all 6 fixed**)
| # | How serious | What | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **High** | **Leave types:** open / edit / **delete** by id have no company check (`controllers/leaveTypeController.js`). HR in company X can rename or delete company Y's leave types. The duplicate-code check is also across all companies. | X's HR renamed, then deleted, Y's leave type (200) |
| 2 | **High** | **HR Policy documents:** upload and remove have **no role check and no company check** (`routes/hrPolicy.routes.js`, `controllers/hrPolicyController.js`). **Anyone signed in** can replace or delete any company's policy document; the old file is deleted from the disk. | X's Sales Employee removed Y's policy document, and their own company's (200) |
| 3 | **Medium** | **HR Expenses:** no role check (`routes/hrExpenseRoutes.js`); limited to the company. Any employee can list, add, edit or delete their company's HR expense records. | A Sales Employee listed (200) and added (201) an HR expense |
| 4 | Low | **Holidays:** open by id has no company check (`getHolidayById`). Anyone can read another company's holiday by its id. Edit / delete are checked. | X's Sales Employee opened Y's holiday (200) |
| 5 | Low–medium | **Document status:** the company check is skipped when the document has no company saved (`setDocumentStatus`). On dev, 2 of 18 documents have none. HR of another company can verify / reject those. | X's HR rejected Y's document without a company (200) |
| 6 | Low (note) | **HR dashboard figures:** no role check; own company only. Any employee can read the head count, pending leaves and open jobs. | A Sales Employee read them (200) |

## Fixed (26 Nov, the user chose all 6)
1. **Leave types** (`controllers/leaveTypeController.js`): open / edit / delete by id within the caller's company (the Super Admin: any); the duplicate-code check within the company.
2. **HR Policy documents** (`routes/hrPolicy.routes.js`, `controllers/hrPolicyController.js`): HR / the Company Admin / the Super Admin, checked before the upload so a refused file is never saved; the policy must be their company's (a refused upload's file is deleted); a removed file is only ever looked for in the policy folder.
3. **HR Expenses** (`routes/hrExpenseRoutes.js`): HR / the Company Admin / the Super Admin.
4. **Holidays** (`getHolidayById`): another company's is "not found", as on edit / delete.
5. **Document status** (`setDocumentStatus`): with no company on the document, its employee's company is used; a caller without a company gets nothing.
6. **HR dashboard figures** (`routes/hrmsDashboard.routes.js`): HR / the Company Admin / a Manager / the Super Admin (the two dashboards that show them).
