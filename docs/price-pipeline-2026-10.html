Price Pipeline · Samtek ERP · 30 Oct 2026

Kharid ke rate se Machine ke MRP tak

Ek page me poora hisaab: raw material ka rate kaise banta hai, wo BOM me kaise jaata hai, outsource (job work) aur Production ka kharcha kaise judta hai, aur sab milkar Machine ka MRP / Sale Price kaise banta hai jo Sales dekhta hai. Ye sab apne aap hota hai: koi page kholna nahi padta. Aap har hissa padhkar bataiye ki ye aapki zaroorat se milta hai ya kuch badalna hai. Aapke sawaal sabse neeche hain.

Ek nazar me The pipeline at a glance

  1. 1
    Raw material ka rateStore ne PO ka maal receive kiya → us PO line ka asli rate (GST ke bina + PO charges ka hissa)
  2. 2
    BOM ki material lineqty × rate, ya fabrication me weight (kg) × ₹/kg
  3. 3
    Sub Child Part (per piece)material + scrap + (job work + Production cost + expense) ÷ bane hue pieces
  4. 4
    Child PartSub Child Parts + apna material + job work + Production cost / expense
  5. 5
    Machine ki costChild Parts + apna material + job work + Production cost / expense
  6. ₹
    MRP aur Sale PricePricing Value ka profit % aur discount % → Sales yahi padhta hai (quotation, order form)

Job work ka paisa Outsource Order se apne aap aata hai, aur Production apna kharcha apne aakhri in-house step pe likhta hai. Neeche har step detail me.

1. Raw material ka rate Purchase price

Item ka rate = PO line ka rate (GST ke bina) + us line ka PO charges me hissa (line ki value ke hisaab se, GST ke bina) × unit conversion (jaise 1 sheet = 38.25 kg)
  • Kab

    Rate tab badalta hai jab maal aata hai, PO approve hone pe nahi

    • Store ka receive jis PO line ko receive karta hai, rate usi line se banta hai. Purana PO baad me aaye to apna hi rate lagta hai, naye PO ka nahi.
    • PO approve hone se kuch re-price nahi hota.
    • Bina PO ka manual bill: us bill ka rate (pehle jaisa).
    • Accounts ka manual price aur ₹/kg screen: wo bhi turant upar push hota hai.
  • GST

    GST cost me nahi gina jaata (wo wapas claim hota hai)

    • "GST included" rate pe GST % poocha jaata hai aur rate se nikaal diya jaata hai: ₹200 incl. 18% → ₹169.49.
    • Purane quote jinka % pata nahi, wo jaise hain waise gine jaate hain (andaza nahi lagate).
    • Bill, PO total, GST, payment: kuch nahi badla. Ye sirf item ki cost ka hisaab hai.

2. BOM ki material line Material lines

  • Line

    Normal item: qty × rate. Fabrication item (sheet, pipe, angle): weight × ₹/kg

    • Fabrication line ka weight cut size aur density se banta hai, rate us item ka ₹/kg.
    • Sheet Plan wale Sub Child Part ka scrap bhi usi ₹/kg pe: rate badla to scrap ki cost bhi badalti hai.
    • Fabrication Master me density / material badla (weight badla) → wo bhi price push chalata hai (28 Oct).

3. Job work (outsource) ka kharcha Outsource cost

Purchase ko receive pe kuch type nahi karna. Paisa pehle se Outsource Order me hai (per piece / poori line / per job work / per minute / per hour), wahi padha jaata hai.

Ek trip ka paisa = line amount ÷ line qty × QC me pass hue pieces (GST ke bina) + Outsource Order ke charges (packing, testing…) ka hissa, line amount ke hisaab se
  • Niyam

    Sirf accepted pieces ka paisa

    • QC ne jo reject kiye, wo bill se kat jaate hain, isliye cost me nahi.
    • Redo nayi trip hai, apne rate pe (vendor free me kare to ₹0).
    • Dispatch ki kiraye ki gaadi (freight) abhi cost me nahi, jaise PO ke maal me bhi nahi.
  • Level

    Child Part / Machine: har unit ka apna. Sub Child Part: poora batch ÷ bane pieces

    • Child Part / Machine ki har unit jitni trips pe gayi, sabka paisa jodti hai (units alag-alag step pe alag batch me ja sakti hain).
    • Sub Child Part: batch ki saari trips ka paisa ÷ jo pieces stock me gaye. Vendor ko paisa dene ke baad scrap hue pieces ki cost bache hue pieces pe bant jaati hai.
  • Time

    Per minute / per hour step ka time abhi nahi likha

    • Order aur stock pehle jaise poore hote hain; sirf part ki cost rukti hai.
    • BOM page pe dikhta hai "job-work cost waiting for the time on OSO-…".
    • Purchase time likhe (jab bill ki row bhi bharti hai) → cost turant update.

4. Production ka apna kharcha Production cost and expense

  • Kab

    Production apna cost + expense apne aakhri in-house step pe likhta hai

    • Aakhri step in-house hai → wahi step (pehle jaisa).
    • Aakhri step(s) outsource hain → unse pehle wala Production ka aakhri step.
    • Poora outsource (koi in-house step nahi) → koi nahi poochta, Production cost ₹0.
    • Sub Child Part: poore batch ka total ek baar; per piece me ÷ bane pieces.

Teeno number (job work, Production cost, Production expense) unit chalte waqt jama hote rehte hain aur BOM me ek saath tab jaate hain jab sach me aakhri step khatam hota hai. Kaun karta hai:

Naya build purane ki jagah leta hai

  • Sabse naya poora build jeetta hai: har bana unit / batch part ki cost apni asli cost se badal deta hai (average nahi).
  • Lekin naya build jiska time (per minute / per hour) abhi baaki hai, wo pichhle poore build ki cost nahi hatata, taaki Sales ko kabhi kam price na dikhe. Time aate hi wo replace karta hai.
  • QC ne aakhri step reject kiya → redo pass hone tak koi push nahi.

5. Teeno level ki cost Part costs

Sub Child Part (1 piece) = material + scrap + (job work + Production cost + expense) ÷ bane pieces Child Part = Sub Child Parts + apna material + job work + Production cost + expense Machine = Child Parts + apna material + job work + Production cost + expense MRP = Machine cost + profit % Sale Price = MRP − discount % (discount MRP pe lagta hai, cost pe nahi)
  • Kahan

    Kis number ko kaun padhta hai

    • Machine: cost, MRP, Sale Price item pe save hote hain. Sales (Product Master, lead quotation, order form ka minimum bill) yahi padhta hai.
    • Child Part: sirf cost save hoti hai (MRP nahi, kyunki abhi wo becha nahi jaata). Neeche sawaal 5 dekhiye.
    • profit % / discount %: Company Admin → Pricing Value. Inhe badalne se sirf us item ka MRP / Sale Price dobara banta hai.
    • Profit / discount khaali ho to 0 maana jaata hai: MRP = cost.

6. Price push: teen raaste upar The cascade

Koi bhi cost badli to badlav har raaste se Machine ke price tak ek saath chadhta hai. Kisi ko BOM page kholne ki zaroorat nahi.

Push ke niyam

  • Sirf tab likhta hai jab number sach me badla.
  • Har part ek badlav me ek hi baar (kabhi ghoomta nahi).
  • Jis part ki abhi koi cost nahi, use chhod deta hai: kabhi ₹0 nahi karta.
  • Price me koi gadbad ho to bhi stock / QC / purchase ka kaam kabhi wapas nahi hota.
  • BOM page kholne pe bhi refresh hota hai (safety net).
  • Pehle bheji gayi quotations nahi badalti.

7. Make or buy: khareeda hua part Bought parts

Har Child Part / Sub Child Part ke do price hamesha rehte hain: made cost (BOM se, push se taaza) aur bought cost (aakhri asli kharid, step 1 jaisa, ya Accounts ka estimate). R&D ka radio (Made / Bought) tay karta hai upar wala BOM kaunsa price use kare.

8. Ek poora example Worked example

Test data (Fan / BP816), GST 18% bahar. Rate badalne ka asar neeche.

  • SCP

    Fan Blade (6 pcs) aur Fan Bush (2 pcs)

    • Fan Bush (poora outsource): cutting ₹25 × 2 = ₹50 + drilling ₹5 × 4 hole × 2 = ₹40 → ₹90 ÷ 2 = ₹45 / piece job work.
    • Fan Blade: laser cutting poori line ₹90 ÷ 6 = ₹15 + Production ki drilling ₹60 ÷ 6 = ₹10 → ₹25 / piece processing.
    • Fan Blade ka material: 0.024 kg × ₹170.28 = ₹4.09; scrap 0.096 kg × ₹170.28 = ₹16.35 (SS sheet ke naye rate se apne aap).
  • CP

    Fan (Child Part, 1 unit)

    • Outsource: OSO-0005 welding ₹150 + grinding ₹100, OSO-0006 pre ₹80 + post ₹60 → job work ₹390.
    • Coating Production ka aakhri in-house step → wahin cost ₹200 + expense ₹50 = ₹250.
    • Fan = 6 × Fan Blade + 2 × Fan Bush + apna material + ₹390 + ₹250.
  • M

    BP816 (Machine): cost ₹4,000, profit 20% → MRP ₹4,800

    • Fan ki cost badli → BP816 ki cost aur MRP turant badalte hain, koi page khole bina.

Abhi kya nahi hota Not covered yet

Ye jaan-boojhkar bahar rakha hai, aapka faisla chahiye

  • Freight: PO ke maal aur outsource dono me apni / kiraye ki gaadi ka kharcha cost me nahi.
  • GST cost me nahi (input credit maana hai).
  • Purana data: push 24 Oct se chalta hai. Usse pehle badle rate upar nahi chadhe (jaise kuch BOM lines jo jodte waqt ke rate pe hain). Idea: ek baar "sab dobara price karo" run + button.
  • Stock value: stock report qty × cost se value nikalti hai; Sub Child Parts aur khareede raw material wahan abhi ₹0 dikhte hain.
  • Manpower cost: baad me. Production ka likha number tab ghante × rate banega; baaki pipeline wahi.
